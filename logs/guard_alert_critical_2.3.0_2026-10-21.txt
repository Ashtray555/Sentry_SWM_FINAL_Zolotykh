=== E. 3 wrong PINs -> ALARM, and attempt-counter reset (GD-3, GD-4)  ** BUG-06 **
First run in this session after `guard arm` (+ one wrong `guard pin`): the first wrong `guard disarm 0000` printed "Attempts: 3/3" and raised ALARM (relay OPEN, VERBOSE, CRITICAL #15). Further wrong PINs printed 4/3 and 5/3 while in ALARM.
Isolation, each case: correct PIN disarm first, then guard arm, then ONE wrong `guard disarm 0000`:
  case1 (after a wrong `guard pin`) -> E803 Attempts: 6/3 -> ALARM
  case2 (no prior pin command)      -> E803 Attempts: 7/3 -> ALARM
  case3 (after E807 while armed)    -> E803 Attempts: 8/3 -> ALARM
PRD GD-4: counter starts from zero at every `guard arm` and resets after a correct PIN. Observed: counter is cumulative (never reset by guard arm or by the correct PIN in between), so after the first 3 lifetime wrong PINs every single wrong PIN raises ALARM.
